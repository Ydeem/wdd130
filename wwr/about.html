Wild River Rafting Co.

At Wild River Rafting Co., our mission is simple: connect people with the thrill of the river and the beauty of the wild places it flows through. Every trip is guided by experienced, safety-first rafters who want you to have the adventure of a lifetime.

History

Wild River Rafting Co. started as a small weekend guide service run by a handful of friends who loved the water more than anything else.

Over the years, word of mouth turned that small crew into a full outfitter with a fleet of rafts, a team of certified guides, and a reputation for unforgettable trips. We're still local, still small enough to know our guests by name, and still just as obsessed with the river as we were on day one.
