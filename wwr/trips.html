Rafting Trips

Whether it's your first time in a raft or your fiftieth, there's a run on our river for you. Every trip includes a certified guide, all safety gear, and a riverside meal.

Our Most Popular Trips

Half Day Adventure

Our most popular introduction to rafting. You'll float a scenic four hour stretch of river with friendly Class II and III rapids, plenty of time to swim in the calm pools, and a snack stop on a sandy beach. Perfect for families and first timers.

  • Class II–III
  • Length 4 hours
  • Ages 7+
  • Price $79

Sunrise Rapids

Launch at first light and watch the canyon walls turn gold as you paddle through lively Class III water. The full day trip includes a hot breakfast on the river, a hike to a hidden waterfall, and a riverside lunch.

  • Class III
  • Length Full day
  • Ages 10+
  • Price $149

Big Mallard

Named for the giant standing wave at its heart, Big Mallard is a full day of continuous Class IV rapids for paddlers who want real excitement. You'll scout the biggest drops with your guide before running them, and paddle hard the whole way.

  • Class IV
  • Length Full day
  • Ages 14+
  • Price $189

Lava Falls Overnight

Two days deep in the red rock canyon, ending with a run through Lava Falls, our most famous rapid. Camp under the stars on a private beach while your guides cook dinner. Tents and meals are included, just bring a sleeping bag.

  • Class IV–V
  • Length 2 days
  • Ages 16+
  • Price $429
